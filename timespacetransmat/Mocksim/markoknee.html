Mark O’Knee’s Folkstone Protocols

Mark O’Knee will restage a famous journey between Bath and Folkstone ‘U’K over 100 days beginning on 25 November 2025. Each day, a new entry will appear from one of 100 stations, towns, or villages on the route. Using a Travel Diaries app, the trip will unfold as a travel journal. It will remain unclear whether O’Knee is actually visiting the destinations or fabricating the visits.
