RAVE CINEMA

An Immersive Audiovisual Performance by Yichu Li.

Concept Overview

RAVE CINEMA is a hybrid cinematic ritual—an immersive audiovisual live performance merging AI-coded visuals, techno soundscapes, and feminist mythology. It interrogates how we process identity, power, and presence in a posthuman era, inviting the audience into a space where the boundaries between body, image, and machine dissolve.

Inspired by underground raves, experimental cinema, and techno-feminism, the work operates as a ritual of resistance and re‑imagining. Performed live, it unfolds as a nonlinear narrative of digital embodiment and transformation.

Translation — shifting codes across bodies and images.

Teleportation — ritual portals and time slips.

Science Fiction — speculative mythologies for the posthuman.
